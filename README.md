# Minecraft Misogynist Unexpansion

A [Horizon](https://playhorizon.dev) mod that takes the game back towards how it was.

## What it does

- **Takes out mobs.** By default: dolphins, foxes, sniffers, copper golems, allays, turtles, bees,
  parrots, husks, strays, polar bears, pandas, camels, llamas, phantoms, frogs, glow squids,
  striders, camel husks, armadillos, axolotls, happy ghasts, sulfur cubes, nautiluses, trader llamas,
  zombie nautiluses, bogged, parched, breezes, creakings, tadpoles and goats. None spawn, and those
  already in a world are gone when they load.
- **No new looks for old mobs.** Cows, pigs and chickens only come in their original look (no cold
  or warm ones), and wolves only as the original wolf with its classic sounds (none of the eight
  newer wolves). Old ones are changed back too, checked every two seconds.
- **The old babies.** Every baby the redesign changed is drawn as before, with the grown model,
  textures and overlays at the old sizes: villagers (half size, in their biome's clothes), zombies,
  husks, drowned, zombie villagers, piglins and zombified piglins (big head, small body), cows,
  mooshrooms, pigs, sheep (with their wool's colour), chickens, wolves (variant, collar), cats
  (variant, collar), ocelots, rabbits, hoglins, zoglins, horses (colour and markings), donkeys,
  mules, skeleton and zombie horses, squid and glow squid. The mobs this mod removes by default keep
  the new look if you bring them back.
- **No Dried Ghast, no Pigstep.** The Dried Ghast has no recipe and drops nothing when broken; a
  Pigstep disc in any chest or loot is one of C418's original discs instead, at random.
- **Fishing as it was.** Treasure can be caught anywhere again, not only in open water (a pond fishes
  like the ocean), and tropical fish come 7 times in 100 instead of 2.
- **No sculk in new terrain.** Sculk patches (the deep dark's sculk floors, sensors and shriekers)
  and loose sculk veins no longer generate; the sculk is crafted instead (below). Terrain already
  generated keeps its sculk, and ancient city buildings still have the sculk built into them.
- **No leaf litter, wildflowers or pink petals generate.** None in new terrain; those already in a
  world stay. Craft them instead: four leaf litter from any leaves, four wildflowers from a
  dandelion and an oxeye daisy, four pink petals from a pink tulip and cherry leaves. A furnace still
  makes them too: leaf litter from leaves, wildflowers from a flower (dandelion, poppy, blue orchid,
  allium, azure bluet, red, orange or white tulip, oxeye daisy, cornflower, lily of the valley),
  pink petals from a pink tulip.
- **Kelp only in warm, lukewarm and frozen oceans.** None grows in ordinary or cold oceans any
  more, and warm and frozen oceans (deep ones too) get it. Only newly generated ocean changes: kelp
  already in the world stays. Two kelp are crafted from seagrass and bone meal.
- **Old animal sounds.** Pigs, cows, chickens, wolves and cats make their classic sounds: no big or
  mini pigs, moody cows, picky chickens, royal cats or angry, big, cute, grumpy, puglin and sad
  wolves, and babies sound as they used to (the grown sounds at a baby's voice).
- **Wolves only the original colour.** Every wolf is the original pale one, new or old, and is drawn
  so even on a server that hasn't got this mod.
- **Enchanting costs the full level it asks for.** A level 30 enchantment takes 30 levels, a level 8
  one 8, not vanilla's 1 to 3. The table's tooltip still shows vanilla's 1 to 3.
- **Shields block half.** A raised shield takes 50% of a hit's damage; the other half still hurts.
- **Piercing and Multishot together.** A crossbow can have both, from the table, an anvil or `/enchant`.
- **Crossbow rockets.** A firework rocket shot from a crossbow never hurts the player who shot it
  (it still hurts everyone and everything else in its blast), and a crossbow loads rockets from
  anywhere in the inventory, as it does arrows: held ammo first, then the inventory's first arrows
  or rockets, so a crossbow in the off hand needs no rocket in the main hand.
- **Netherite in an anvil.** Put a diamond tool, weapon or piece of armour in an anvil with a
  netherite ingot for its netherite kind, keeping its enchantments, name and wear: no smithing
  template needed (1 level).
- **Deepslate digs faster.** Only 15% slower than stone instead of twice as slow; cobbled
  deepslate likewise against cobblestone, deepslate ores against the stone ores, and polished
  deepslate, its bricks and tiles against stone bricks.
- **Powder snow holds you up, slows you and freezes you.** You walk on it, leather boots or not,
  and never sink in, even landing on it from high up. It slows you as soul sand does, and after
  15 seconds standing on it you are frozen and take its damage every two seconds (leather armour
  still keeps the cold out).
- **Right-click harvesting.** Right-clicking a fully grown crop (wheat, carrots, potatoes,
  beetroots, nether wart, cocoa) harvests it and replants it: it drops what breaking it would, less
  the one item it is replanted with (no Fortune). Sneak to place blocks on crops as usual.
- **Pick up villagers.** Sneak and right-click a villager to pick it up: it becomes an item, a
  "Villager" ("Baby Villager" for a baby, or its own name) that says its profession and level. Use
  it on a block to set the villager down again, as it was: its trades, experience, job and gossip.
  It goes into your empty hand, or your inventory; with no room, the villager stays. A sleeping
  villager can't be picked up. The idea is
  [Easy Villagers](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/easy-villagers)' (Max Henkel),
  written anew for Horizon.
- **Key Binds search by key.** The search box on the Key Binds screen finds a binding by the key
  it is on as well as by its name: type `r`, `shift` or `mouse` to see what uses it. The idea is
  [Controlling](https://github.com/jaredlll08/Controlling)'s (Jaredlll08, MIT), written anew for
  Horizon. (Horizon already has Mouse Tweaks built in: Horizon Settings, Extras.)
- **The old water.** Water looks as it did in beta: its own deep blue textures (still and
  flowing, drawn for this mod), beta's one blue in every biome (no swamp-green), only warm and
  lukewarm oceans leaning a little towards their lighter blue, and the old water fog underwater: dark blue, darker the less sky reaches you, and
  thick, gone 16 blocks out. The fog's colours and distance are
  [Nostalgic Tweaks](https://github.com/Adrenix/Nostalgic-Tweaks)' old water fog (Adrenix),
  written anew for Horizon. The colour and fog can be turned off (below).
- **What the removed mobs gave, made another way:**

  | Item | How |
  |---|---|
  | Honey bottle | slime ball, sugar and a glass bottle (shapeless) |
  | Honeycomb | a honey bottle, smelted |
  | Glow ink sac | ink sac and glowstone dust (shapeless) |
  | Ochre froglight | glowstone ringed with eight slime balls |
  | Verdant froglight | a sea lantern ringed with eight slime balls |
  | Pearlescent froglight | a shroomlight ringed with eight slime balls |
  | Potion of Dolphin's Grace (3:00) | glass bottle, cod and prismarine crystals (shapeless) |
  | Fish eggs (turtle eggs) | nine tropical fish |
  | Fish scales (turtle scutes) | dropped by tropical fish, nine times in ten |
  | Sculk | dirt and a bottle o' enchanting (shapeless) |
  | 4 sculk veins | one sculk |
  | Sculk sensor | `V V` / `SRS`: two sculk veins over sculk, redstone, sculk |
  | Sculk catalyst | `BXB` / `SSS` / `SSS`: bone, bottle o' enchanting, bone over six sculk |
  | Sculk shrieker | `B B` / `SXS` / `SSS`: two bones, then sculk, bottle o' enchanting, sculk, over three sculk (never summons a warden) |
  | Bottle o' enchanting | any mob head or skull: skeleton, wither skeleton, zombie, creeper, piglin or dragon |

  The sculk and bottle o' enchanting recipes come from the native mod, so the content mod's file
  stays the same as servers that check it know.

## Choosing which mobs go

`removed_mobs.cfg`, in the mod's config folder, is written the first time the game runs. It lists
only the mobs above, each `removed` or `kept`:

```
dolphin = removed
fox = kept
```

A saved change takes hold within ten seconds. Nothing else (zombies, creepers...) can be removed.

## The old water's switches

`old_water.cfg`, beside it, turns the old water's colour and fog `on` or `off` (both on at
first); a saved change takes hold within ten seconds:

```
colour = on
fog = on
```

The old textures come with the content mod: turn it off in Options > Mods for vanilla's (and then
set `colour = off`, or vanilla's grey water stays untinted).

## Two parts

Install both, then press Build in the launcher:

- **`misogynist_unexpansion_native`** (`.hmod`, native): the code. A native mod: it runs with your
  account's permissions, so the launcher asks before building it.
- **`misogynist_unexpansion`** (`.hmod`): the first recipes, the potion and the old water
  textures.

## Not possible yet

- A brewing recipe: a mod can't add one (they live in vanilla's own folder), so the Potion of
  Dolphin's Grace is crafted.
- Nether fossils still place a Dried Ghast now and then: the prebuilt game lets no mod change that
  piece of world generation. It can't be crafted, and a broken one drops nothing.
- On a server, the babies' looks (a villager's clothes, a horse's colour, a sheep's wool) come
  from the server a few times a second, so it needs this mod too.
- Babies are drawn without held items or armour, and a sitting or lying wolf or cat baby stands.
- The old water lighting (water dimming light by 3 a block, not 1): Horizon has no hook into its
  light engine for it. Underwater particles, drips and ambience are vanilla's too, and Respiration
  doesn't brighten the old fog.
- The water bucket and potions keep vanilla's look.

## Building

`gen_content.py <folder>` writes the content mod; `cargo horizon-mod build` packs it. The native
mod is `native-sdk/` (SDK packages, from 0.17.0+sdk.1), packed with `cargo horizon-mod build` and
built by the Horizon launcher (`native-build`), pinned to one Horizon revision. `native/` is the old
`.hnmod` form, up to 0.17.0.
